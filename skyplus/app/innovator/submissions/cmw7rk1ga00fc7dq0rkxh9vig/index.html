Your submitted answers (version 1)

Your idea

Name of your idea
Spaza Pay
Your idea in two sentences
Offline-first mobile point of sale that lets small tuck shops accept mobile money and track stock on any phone.
Sector
Financial Services

Problem and solution

What problem are you solving, and for whom?
Most small shops in Botswana run on cash and paper. Owners cannot show sales history, so they cannot get credit.
How does your solution work?
A USSD and Android app that records every sale, accepts Orange Money, MyZaka and Smega, and syncs when there is signal.
What is new about it compared with what exists in Botswana today?
Works fully offline and builds a verifiable sales record lenders can use for credit scoring.

Market and impact

Who will pay, and how many customers could you reach in 3 years?
Around 15,000 informal retailers. Free basic tier; paid stock and credit features.
Jobs you expect to create in 3 years
14
How does it support Botswana's development goals?
Financial inclusion for informal traders, many of them women.

Readiness

How far along are you?
Prototype built
Funding requested (BWP)
300000
How would you use the funding?
Payment integrations, field agents and a credit pilot with a microlender.

Your team
