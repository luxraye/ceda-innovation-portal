Your idea

Name of your idea
Thaba Feeds
Your idea in two sentences
Turning brewery waste and mopane worm by-products into affordable protein feed for poultry farmers.
Sector
Agriculture & Agro-processing

Problem and solution

What problem are you solving, and for whom?
Poultry feed is the largest cost for small broiler farmers and is mostly imported from South Africa.
How does your solution work?
We dry and blend spent grain with insect protein into pellets that meet broiler nutritional standards at 20% lower cost.
What is new about it compared with what exists in Botswana today?
Uses local waste streams that are currently dumped, creating a circular feed supply.

Market and impact

Who will pay, and how many customers could you reach in 3 years?
Over 3,000 small poultry producers. Sales through agro-dealers in Gaborone, Mahalapye and Francistown.
Jobs you expect to create in 3 years
30
How does it support Botswana's development goals?
Import substitution, waste reduction and cheaper chicken for households.
