Submission (version 1)

Your idea

Name of your idea
AquaGrow Fish Farms
Your idea in two sentences
Modular backyard aquaponics kits that let households grow tilapia and vegetables with 90% less water.
Sector
Agriculture & Agro-processing

Problem and solution

What problem are you solving, and for whom?
Fresh fish is expensive and scarce away from the Okavango, and urban households have little space or water to farm.
How does your solution work?
A 1,000-litre kit with fish tank and grow beds, plus fingerlings and training through WhatsApp.
What is new about it compared with what exists in Botswana today?
Locally fabricated from recycled drums, priced for households, with a buy-back scheme for harvested fish.

Market and impact

Who will pay, and how many customers could you reach in 3 years?
Peri-urban households and schools around Gaborone and Francistown.
Jobs you expect to create in 3 years
10
How does it support Botswana's development goals?
Household nutrition and income, and water-efficient food production.

Readiness

How far along are you?
Piloting with users
Funding requested (BWP)
250000
How would you use the funding?
Fabrication jigs, 50 pilot kits and a fingerling supply partnership.

Your team

Who is on your team and what do they bring?
Founder trained in aquaculture in Zambia; partner runs a welding workshop.
