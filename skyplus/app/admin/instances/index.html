Why templates and instances?
A new call is data, not code: a form, a rubric and stages. Every idea submitted to it becomes a locked instance with mandatory fields checked, a sealed fingerprint and a receipt the founder keeps, so nobody, including CEDA staff, can change it unnoticed.
