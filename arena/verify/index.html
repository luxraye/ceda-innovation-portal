Arena.CEDA Innovation Range

Arena is a prototype of the CEDA Innovation Portal: a national range where youth-owned Botswana companies sharpen their ideas, climb a transparent Innovator Index, and bring their best work to CEDA calls.

Built by Local Grow (Pty) Ltd (UIN BW00009995803) for the CEDA Innovation Portal Hackathon, November 2026. Not an official CEDA website. All people, companies and ideas shown are fictional demo data.
