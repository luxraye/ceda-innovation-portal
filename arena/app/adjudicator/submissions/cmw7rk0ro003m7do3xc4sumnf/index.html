Submission (version 2)

Your idea

Name of your idea
Pula Sense
Your idea in two sentences
Solar-powered soil sensors that text smallholder farmers when and how much to irrigate. Built for borehole-fed plots in Kgalagadi and Kweneng.
Sector
Agriculture & Agro-processing

Problem and solution

What problem are you solving, and for whom?
Smallholder horticulture farmers over-water by 30 to 50% because they irrigate on a fixed schedule. Borehole diesel and electricity are their biggest cost after seed, and water tables are falling.
How does your solution work?
A P450 probe measures soil moisture and temperature every hour and sends readings over a low-power network to our gateway. Farmers receive a Setswana or English SMS each morning: water now, wait, or how many minutes to run the pump. Pilot results: 14 farms in Tsabong used 38% less borehole water over 10 weeks, and 11 of 14 farmers said they would pay for the service.
What is new about it compared with what exists in Botswana today?
Existing sensors cost over P3,000 and need a smartphone and data. Ours costs a seventh of that, runs on a coin-sized solar cell and works on any phone through SMS.

Market and impact

Who will pay, and how many customers could you reach in 3 years?
About 8,000 horticulture smallholders in Botswana, plus NGOs and the Ministry's ISPAAD-supported farmers. We sell probes at P450 and a P25 monthly SMS plan. Target: 1,200 farmers in three years.
Jobs you expect to create in 3 years
18
How does it support Botswana's development goals?
Cuts water and energy use, raises yields for local vegetables, and supports food security by reducing reliance on imported produce.
