Submission (version 1)

Your idea

Name of your idea
KgomoTrack
Your idea in two sentences
Low-cost GPS ear tags and a herd app that help cattle owners find lost animals and prove ownership to buyers.
Sector
Agriculture & Agro-processing

Problem and solution

What problem are you solving, and for whom?
Farmers lose days searching for cattle at cattle posts, and stock theft costs the sector millions of pula each year.
How does your solution work?
Solar ear tags report location twice a day over LoRa gateways mounted at cattle posts. Owners see their herd on a map and get alerts when animals leave a set area.
What is new about it compared with what exists in Botswana today?
Works without mobile coverage, links to the national livestock identification system and costs under P150 per animal.

Market and impact

Who will pay, and how many customers could you reach in 3 years?
Commercial and communal farmers in the North West and Ghanzi. Subscription per tag plus gateway sales to syndicates.
Jobs you expect to create in 3 years
15
How does it support Botswana's development goals?
Less theft, better traceability for beef exports and time saved for farming families.

Prototype built
