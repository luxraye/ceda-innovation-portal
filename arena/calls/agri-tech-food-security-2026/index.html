Agri-Tech & Food Security Challenge 2026

Technology and new business models that help Botswana grow, store and sell more of its own food.

The challenge
Food import substitution is a national priority. This call funds youth-led ideas across the agricultural value chain: inputs, production, storage, processing and market access. Applications closed and panels are now reviewing. The committee will announce winners after pitch sessions.
How you'll be judged
Σ weights = 100

Every evaluator scores these criteria. Publishing them up front is part of keeping Arena fair.

  • Innovation and originality25%

    How new the idea is for Botswana, and how hard it would be to copy.

  • Market potential20%

    Size of the opportunity, willingness to pay and a credible route to customers.

  • Feasibility and readiness20%

    Can this team build and run it with the funding asked for?

  • Socio-economic impact20%

    Jobs for youth, inclusion and contribution to national development priorities.

  • Team capability15%

    Skills, commitment and track record of the founders.

The stages your idea will pass through
  1. 1
    Eligibility screening Eligibility screening

    CEDA confirms the company, youth shareholding and documents. Usually within 5 working days.

  2. 2
    Panel evaluation Panel evaluation

    At least two independent evaluators score the idea blind against the published criteria.

  3. 3
    Committee adjudication Committee adjudication

    The CEDA innovation committee reviews ranked ideas, may invite a pitch, and decides.

  4. 4
    Award Award

    Winners receive funding and enter CEDA's mentoring and aftercare programme.

Who can apply

  • Batswana citizens aged 18 to 35
  • A company registered with CIPA
  • More than 50% of shares held by youth
  • No application fee

What you'll need

15 required answers. Your draft saves as you go.

  • Name of your idea
  • Your idea in two sentences
  • Sector
  • What problem are you solving, and for whom?
  • How does your solution work?
  • What is new about it compared with what exists in Botswana today?
  • Who will pay, and how many customers could you reach in 3 years?
  • Jobs you expect to create in 3 years
  • How does it support Botswana's development goals?
  • How far along are you?
  • Funding requested (BWP)
  • How would you use the funding?
  • Who is on your team and what do they bring?
  • Pitch deck
  • CIPA certificate of incorporation
