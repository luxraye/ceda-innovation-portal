Scams & feesTebogo Ramasedi · 11 d ago

Nobody can sell you a place in a CEDA call

This portal never asks for a fee to apply, to be scored or to be shortlisted, and evaluators and staff will never ask you for money or gifts. If anyone offers to 'fast-track' your application for a fee, it is a scam. Report it to CEDA and do not pay.

Protecting your ideaTebogo Ramasedi · 12 d ago

Protect your idea before you share it widely

An idea on its own is hard to protect. What you can protect is your brand, your code and designs, and inventions that are new. In Botswana, patents, trademarks and industrial designs are registered with CIPA. If you plan to patent an invention, file before you publish or pitch it in public, because public disclosure can stop it counting as new.

Scams & feesTebogo Ramasedi · 1 d ago

Be careful with 'investors' who want fees up front

Real investors make money when you do. Someone who asks for a due-diligence fee, a 'registration' fee or a deposit before investing is very likely running a scam.

Protecting your ideaTebogo Ramasedi · 2 d ago

Choosing a name? Check it's free first

Before you print anything, check that your business name and logo aren't already registered or in use in your sector. Rebranding after launch is expensive, and a clash can lead to a legal letter.

Data & privacyKgosi Mothibi · 5 d ago

Collecting customer data? The Data Protection Act applies to you

If your product stores names, phone numbers, locations or health information, Botswana's Data Protection Act, 2024 applies. Ask for consent, collect only what you need, and keep it secure. Evaluators look for this in digital ideas.

Pitch pitfallsTebogo Ramasedi · 6 d ago

What to share in a pitch, and what to keep back

Panels need to understand how your solution works, not the exact recipe. Explain the problem, the approach and the results. Keep source code, supplier pricing and detailed formulas for due diligence, under an agreement, after you're shortlisted.

Contracts & co-foundersKgosi Mothibi · 7 d ago

Put co-founder agreements in writing early

Most co-founder disputes come from things nobody wrote down: who owns what, who does what, and what happens if someone leaves. A short shareholders' agreement now is far cheaper than a fight later, and CEDA checks youth shareholding at screening.
