Application

What problem are you solving?
Cattle theft and straying cost farmers in the North-East heavily, and BAITS tags don't give live location.
Your solution
Solar ear tags that report location twice a day over LoRa to a village gateway, with SMS alerts.
What makes it new or better?
Tags under P150 each and gateways shared by a whole village.
Who will pay for it?
Farmers' associations and insurers.
