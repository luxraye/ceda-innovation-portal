Application

What problem are you solving?
Rural BGCSE candidates have little data to use online revision tools.
Your solution
A low-cost offline revision library on USB and SD cards, updated monthly through school visits.
What makes it new or better?
Works with no data at all; tracks progress offline and syncs when a teacher's phone connects.
Who will pay for it?
Schools and parents, sponsored by local businesses.
