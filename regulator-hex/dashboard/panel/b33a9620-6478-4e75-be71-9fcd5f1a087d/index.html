What problem are you solving?
Tuck shops lose stock credit because suppliers can't see their sales history.
Your solution
A USSD and WhatsApp ledger that builds a sales record and lets wholesalers offer 14-day stock credit.
What makes it new or better?
Works on any phone; credit scoring from daily sales instead of bank statements.
Who will pay for it?
Wholesalers pay per active shop; shops use it free.
Your team and their skills
Kabelo (software engineer, ex-bank), Lesedi (field sales, 80 shops signed).
