Application

What problem are you solving?
Botswana imports nearly all its floor and wall tiles while bottle glass goes to landfill.
Your solution
Crush and fuse collected bottles into decorative tiles in a small kiln in Lobatse.
What makes it new or better?
Uses local waste glass as the main raw material; no clay import needed.
Who will pay for it?
Hardware chains, interior designers and government housing projects.
Your team and their skills
Naledi plus a ceramics graduate and a former brewery logistics manager.
Main district of operation
South-East
Funding requested (Pula)

14
How does this support Botswana's development goals?
Import substitution, recycling and factory jobs for young people.
