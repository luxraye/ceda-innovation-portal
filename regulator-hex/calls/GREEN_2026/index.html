GREEN_2026

Green Energy & Water Innovation Call

Green Energy & Water · Idea stage

Clean energy, water saving and climate adaptation ideas at idea or prototype stage.

How ideas are scored

2 independent evaluators score each criterion from 1 to 5. Scores are weighted to a total out of 100 and averaged.

  • Innovation & originality25%

    Is this new for Botswana? Does it solve the problem in a better way?

  • Market potential20%

    Real customers, willingness to pay, room to grow beyond the first market.

  • Feasibility & team20%

    Can this team build and run it with the money asked for?

  • Impact & jobs20%

    Citizen jobs, inclusion, rural reach, environmental benefit.

  • Fit with CEDA priorities15%

    Economic diversification, digital advancement, sustainable development.

What you'll be asked

  1. What problem are you solving?
  2. Your solution
  3. What makes it new or better?
  4. Who will pay for it?
  5. Your team and their skills
  6. Main district of operation
  7. Funding requested (Pula)
  8. Citizen jobs created in 12 months
  9. How does this support Botswana's development goals?
Closes
11 Dec 2026 (in 33 days)
Funding
Up to P100,000 seed grant and technical support
Who can apply
Citizens aged 18–35, through a CIPA-registered company more than 50% youth-owned
