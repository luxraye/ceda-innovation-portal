From idea to award, in the open

Every application moves through the same five stages. You can see which one yours is in, at any time.

  1. 1

    Submission

    Answer a short form, upload your pitch deck and CIPA certificate, then sign and submit. You get a tamper-proof receipt.

  2. 2

    Review

    CEDA screens for eligibility. Independent evaluators declare conflicts, then score your idea against published criteria.

  3. 3

    Feedback

    If something is missing, CEDA asks for changes. You reply in the portal and resubmit, with no lost emails.

  4. 4

    Approval

    An adjudication panel sees the ranked shortlist, compares every scorecard and decides.

  5. 5

    Award

    Winners are notified at once with the award amount. Everyone gets anonymous evaluator feedback.

One portal, four roles

Everyone sees only what their job needs.

Innovators

Check eligibility, apply, save drafts, follow the five stages, answer CEDA's questions and resubmit.

Evaluators

Declare conflicts, then score ideas blind on weighted criteria, with written feedback for the founder.

Adjudication panel

See a ranked shortlist with every scorecard side by side, spot disagreements, approve and award.

CEDA programme team

Open calls, screen, assign evaluators, chase overdue scores and export participation reports.

Fair by design

Judged on the idea, not on who you know

Young founders often tell us the hardest part is not knowing what happened to their application. Every step here is recorded and explainable.

Blind scoring

Evaluators never see your name, Omang, age or phone number.

Conflicts declared

Each evaluator confirms no link to your company before opening your idea.

Signed receipt

Your answers and documents are hashed and signed, so nobody can change them later.

Feedback for everyone

Win or not, you get the scores and evaluator comments to improve your next pitch.
