About the CEDA Innovation Portal

A single, fair and transparent way for young Batswana to bring ideas to CEDA, and for CEDA to fund the best of them.

Why it exists

The Citizen Entrepreneurial Development Agency (CEDA) funds and mentors citizen-owned businesses. Its innovation challenge invites youth to propose ideas that support economic diversification, digital advancement and sustainable development. Until now, ideas arrived by email and paper, evaluators scored in spreadsheets, and applicants rarely knew where they stood. This portal replaces that with one flow that every applicant, evaluator and panel member can follow.

How judging works

  1. Screening. CEDA checks eligibility and completeness. The portal pre-checks age, citizenship, youth shareholding, funding against the call ceiling and cost per job.
  2. Independent scoring. Each idea goes to two or more evaluators. Each declares any conflict of interest, then scores 1 to 5 on the call's published, weighted criteria and writes feedback.
  3. Shortlist. When every score is in, the idea moves to the adjudication panel automatically. The panel sees a ranked list, every scorecard side by side, and a warning where evaluators disagree by more than 20 points.
  4. Decision. The panel approves, awards with an amount, asks for changes, or declines with a reason. The innovator is notified at once.
  5. Feedback. Every applicant sees their average score per criterion and the evaluators' comments, anonymously.

Fairness and integrity

  • Each document is fingerprinted with SHA-256 in your browser and again on the server. If they differ, the upload is refused.
  • On submission you get a digitally signed receipt covering your answers and every document fingerprint. If anything changes later, verification fails.
  • Role-based access: innovators, evaluators, adjudicators and programme admins each see only what their role needs, enforced on the server.
  • An append-only audit trail records every submission, assignment, declaration, score, decision and download.

Reaching every district

The portal installs on a phone, works on slow connections, offers Setswana, and has a USSD service so founders without data can check eligibility and track applications. Reports break participation down by district, gender and age so CEDA can see who it is not yet reaching.

This is a hackathon prototype built for CEDA's Innovation Portal challenge. It is not an official CEDA service, and demo data is fictional.
