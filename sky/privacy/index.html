Data Protection Act 2024

Privacy notice.

Version 2026.11-v1 · Prototype text for the CEDA hackathon, to be confirmed by CEDA's Data Protection Officer.

Who is responsible

The Citizen Entrepreneurial Development Agency (CEDA) is the data controller for Sky. CEDA's Data Protection Officer can be reached through the privacy centre in your account. You may also complain to the Information and Data Protection Commission.

What we collect and why

  • Your name, contact details, date of birth and district, to confirm eligibility and contact you about your application.
  • Your company's name, CIPA UIN, youth shareholding and sector, to confirm the company qualifies.
  • Your ideas and documents, so CEDA's evaluators and committee can assess them.
  • Gender, used only in anonymous totals so CEDA can see whether its programmes reach everyone.

We do not collect sensitive data such as health, religion or biometrics.

Your consent

  • Process my application (required): CEDA may use the details I give to check eligibility and manage my application. Required to apply.
  • Share my idea with the evaluation panel (required): Named evaluators and the CEDA innovation committee may read my submission under confidentiality. Required to apply.
  • Feature my idea if it wins (optional): CEDA may publish my company name, idea title and summary in its innovation showcase.
  • Tell me about future CEDA programmes (optional): Occasional emails or SMS about funding, training and new calls. Withdraw any time.

Who sees your idea

Only the CEDA programme team, evaluators assigned to your idea and the innovation committee. Evaluators sign a conflict-of-interest declaration and see your idea without your name during blind review. Every view and decision is recorded in an audit trail.

How long we keep it

Applications that are not selected are anonymised 24 months after the call closes. Awarded applications are kept for the life of CEDA's support and the period the law requires. Audit records are kept for 7 years.

Where it is stored

Sky is designed to run on servers in Botswana. Data is not transferred abroad unless the Act allows it. Files are encrypted at rest and all traffic uses HTTPS.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, move, restrict or erase your data, object to processing, and ask for a person to review any decision. Use the privacy centre in your account; CEDA aims to respond within 30 days. If a breach puts your data at risk, we will tell the Commission without delay and, where feasible, within 72 hours, and tell you if the risk is high.

SkyCEDA Innovation Portal

Sky is a prototype of the CEDA Innovation Portal, where youth-owned Botswana companies bring ideas forward and CEDA evaluates, tracks and rewards them in the open.
