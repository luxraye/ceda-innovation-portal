Why Sky exists

Good ideas get lost in inboxes. Sky gives every one a fair path.

How it works

Five stages. You can see every one.

CEDA can change the stages for each call. This is the standard path an idea takes, and the innovator is notified at every step.

  1. 01SubmitYou

    One guided form. Sky checks eligibility and tells you exactly what's missing before you send.

  2. 02ScreenCEDA team

    Company and documents verified. Complete, eligible ideas move on within days, not months.

  3. 03Panel reviewIndependent evaluators

    At least two experts score against published criteria, without seeing your name.

  4. 04CommitteeCEDA innovation committee

    Fair, normalised rankings, and a recorded reason behind every decision.

  5. 05AwardCEDA

    Winners receive funding and support. Everyone receives written feedback.
